Hardware¶

The connectors, addresses, bus settings, timing, power and fluidics of the supported modules, as the driver uses them. These are flow meter modules with a connector, not chips in a package: the diagrams show the connector of the SFM4300 as the reference and the other connectors are tabulated.

Datasheet sections this page relies on
  • Sf05 reset, restart and first read: Sensirion AG SFM3200 1.1, Digital interface (soft reset 80 ms, restart before read), p. 5
  • Sf04 trigger bytes and the hold-master read: Sensirion AG SFM4100 1.4, Digital interface (trigger, clock stretching, address per gas), p. 4
  • Timing (reset, first sample, wake-up, stop): Sensirion AG SFM4300 D1 1.0, 2.4 Timing specification, p. 5
  • Electrical specification and limits: Sensirion AG SFM4300 D1 1.0, 2.3 Electrical specification; 2.7 Absolute maximum ratings, p. 5
  • Connector and pins: Sensirion AG SFM4300 D1 1.0, 2.8 Electrical connection, Table 1, p. 6
  • IRQn and start-up behaviour: Sensirion AG SFM4300 D1 1.0, 3.2 Sensor start-up and warm-up; 3.3 IRQn, p. 8
  • I2C address and bus: Sensirion AG SFM4300 D1 1.0, 4 Digital interface description; 4.1 I2C address, p. 9
  • Soft reset (general call): Sensirion AG SFM4300 D1 1.0, 4.3.7 Soft reset, p. 13
  • Fluidics, fittings and outline: Sensirion AG SFM4300 D1 1.0, 5 Fluidic specifications; 6 Package outline, p. 17

Facts that are not verified

SDA and SCL need external pull-ups to the bus voltage on every part. No document gives a value for the SF06 parts; choose it for your bus capacitance and clock. The ADDR pin of the SFM4300 must never be connected to VDD, and IRQn must stay open when it is not used (never to GND or VDD).

Address collisions matter: 0x2E is the SFM3019, the SFM3304 and the SFM3505, and 0x40 is every SF05 part. Two sensors on one bus need different addresses, and the driver identifies a part only inside the family you configure (Variant::Auto), never across families.

Bus settings¶

Setting Value
Clock 400 kHz typical, 1 MHz maximum for SF06, SFM3304, SFM3505 and SFx6000; 400 kHz for SFM3000/3200/3300/3400; 100 kHz for SFM4200; 120 kHz for SFM4100. 100 kHz (kSafeClockHz) suits every part
Address 7-bit, no register byte; the sensor accepts raw command words
Write START, address + W, command bytes (and an argument word with its CRC-8), STOP
Read START, address + R, words of two bytes each followed by a CRC-8, the master NACKs the last byte, STOP
Poll A normal write of the next real command (the stop command, the SF05 serial pointer, the SF04 register read), repeated until it is acknowledged; the driver never sends a write with no data byte
Repeated START Not used. The documents show a STOP between a command and its read and neither allow nor forbid a repeated START (ambiguity K-RS)

The CRC is CRC-8, polynomial 0x31, no reflection, final XOR 0x00; the initial value is 0xFF for the SF06 families and the SFx6000 and 0x00 for SF05 and SF04. A read is always a whole number of three-byte words. The SF05 parts lock up when the master NACKs the first data byte; the port must never abort a read early.

SF04 (SFM4100) reads need the sensor to hold SCL low while it measures ("hold master"). The port needs the optional ReadStretch() hook for it, and a controller that does not tolerate a stretching slave cannot run that part.

Timing¶

These are the datasheet maxima the table holds. TimingPolicy::Conservative (the default) waits 20 ms after every SF06 reset and wake; TimingPolicy::Datasheet uses the per-part value and needs a known variant.

Family Power-up Soft reset First sample after start Warm-up (accurate) Wake
SFM4300 20 ms 20 ms 12 ms 30 ms 20 ms
SFM3003, 3013, 3019, 3119 2 ms 2 ms 12 ms 30 ms 16 ms
SFM3304, SFM3505 2 ms 2 ms 4 ms 50 ms 2 ms (SFM3505: no sleep)
SFM3000 100 ms 80 ms 30 ms 30 ms -
SFM3200, 3300, 3400 100 ms 80 ms 100 ms 100 ms -
SFM4200 2 ms /RES pulse (unverified width) 100 ms 100 ms -
SFM4100 20 ms 20 ms read when ready (stretch) 500 ms -
SFM6000D [P] 30 ms 30 ms 12 ms 1 s -

After a stop the SF06 parts accept a command within 0.5 ms. The SFM4300 documents 16 ms typical and 20 ms maximum from reset to ready and says the 16 ms holds if SCL is high; the SFM3xxx datasheets say 2 ms for reset but 16 ms typical for a wake from sleep (ambiguity K-TIME). That is why the conservative policy is the default. Under the Datasheet policy a poll gives up a quarter after the figure; Conservative is the supported choice for sleep.

Power and reset¶

  • General-call reset. The soft reset of the SF06 parts is the byte 0x06 sent to address 0x00. It resets every device on the bus that listens. The driver refuses it unless you set Config::allow_general_call_reset, and the port provides WriteGeneralCall(). A sleeping sensor cannot be soft reset: wake it first.
  • Hardware reset. The SFM4200 has a /RES pin (port hook SetResetPin()). The other SF05 parts and the SFM3505 reset by cycling their supply (port hook PowerCycle()). The other parts have no hard reset in the driver: use the soft reset. Without the hook, HardReset() returns Unsupported. Wiring: SDA and SCL must be pulled low at the same time as /RES or while the supply is off (SFM4200 2.3, SFM3505 3.3.5), or the sensor stays powered through the bus lines and does not reset; the port hooks do this and the board must not back-feed the sensor through its pull-ups. See Hard reset wiring.
  • Sleep. The SF06 parts (not the SFM3505) have a sleep mode of about 0.4 µA typical (SFM4300). A wake is a write to the address that the sensor does not acknowledge; the driver repeats the stop command until it is acknowledged.
  • Idle current. 0.055 mA typical, 4.2 mA while measuring (SFM4300, VDD 3.3 V).
  • IRQn. The SFM4300 pulls IRQn low when a result is ready and after a reset until the next start. The driver does not own the pin; use it as an interrupt to call ReadSample().

The dimensions are read off the datasheets' drawings, which print tolerances (for example 92.5 ± 0.75), so treat them as nominal. The SFM4300 is calibrated with the connector up and the flow horizontal. Operating temperature is +5 to +50 °C for the SFM4300 and the SFM3300, +10 to +50 °C for the SFM3200, -20 to +80 °C for the SFM3000 (0.7 to 1.3 bar).
