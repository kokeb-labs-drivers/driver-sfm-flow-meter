Not in the driver¶

Feature Why
SFC controller products (valve, set-point) Owner decision: out of scope; the flow commands are shared, the control commands are not implemented
SFM3020 and SFM3100 Analog interface
SFM5300, SFM5330, SFM5400, SFM5460, SFM5500, SFC5xxx RS485, SHDLC, DeviceNet or IO-Link; no I2C
SHDLC on the SFM6000 A different bus; the I2C reference is the driver's source
Calibration-data and heater commands beyond those listed Not documented in the sources read
An interrupt-driven mode that owns the IRQn pin The driver does not own pins; the pin is the application's

The SFM3003, SFM3013, SFM3019, SFM3119 and SFM3003-CET datasheets repeat the SFM4300 command set and differ in addresses, scale factors and timing; the SFM3003-CET adds only the in-channel gas temperature in the same frame layout.

Reviewed, not supported (evidence)¶

Twelve of the 32 documents describe other products or interfaces; they are in the register so that "not supported" rests on a document: sfm3020, sfm3100, sfm5300_2016, sfx53x0, sfx5400, sfx5460, sfx5500, sfx5_shdlc, sfx6000_shdlc, sfm_eng_guide (engineering background) and the two CRC and I2C application notes that apply to the legacy parts.
